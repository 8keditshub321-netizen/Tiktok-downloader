Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

TikTokSaver is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information to provide our services:

  • URLs you submit: When you paste a TikTok video link, we temporarily process the URL to fetch video information. These URLs are not stored permanently on our servers.
  • Usage data: We may collect anonymous analytics data such as pages visited, browser type, and referral sources to improve our service.
  • No personal accounts: We do not require registration, login, or any personal information such as email addresses, names, or phone numbers.

2. How We Use Your Information

The limited data we collect is used solely to:

  • Process and deliver TikTok video downloads
  • Improve and optimize our website performance
  • Monitor and analyze usage trends to enhance user experience
  • Ensure the security and proper functioning of our service

3. Cookies and Tracking

TikTokSaver uses minimal cookies. We may use:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic website functionality.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site. These collect anonymous data only.

We do not use advertising cookies or sell any data to third-party advertisers.

4. Third-Party Services

We interact with TikTok's servers solely to fetch publicly available video information when you submit a URL. We do not share your data with any other third parties. Our hosting provider may collect standard server logs for security and performance purposes.

5. Data Retention

Submitted TikTok URLs are processed in real-time and are not stored on our servers after the download is complete. Anonymous analytics data may be retained for up to 12 months for service improvement purposes.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect the limited data we handle. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted to or from our service.

7. Children's Privacy

TikTokSaver is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of our service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please visit our Contact Us page.
